autoshorts

A personal video-automation tool.

What autoshorts is

autoshorts is a personal automation tool, built and operated by a single developer, that generates short-form vertical videos — script, narration, and captions — and publishes them to that developer's own YouTube channel. It is not a public product or service: there is no sign-up, and no one other than its developer uses it.

What it does with your Google data

autoshorts connects to the YouTube Data API to manage videos on the developer's own YouTube channel: uploading rendered videos, setting their title, description, tags, and thumbnail, and scheduling when they go public. See the privacy policy for details.
